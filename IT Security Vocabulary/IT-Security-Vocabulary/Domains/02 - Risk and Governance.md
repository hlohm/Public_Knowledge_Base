# Risk & Governance

> Risk vocabulary is the grammar of executive conversations. Once you know how `asset`, `threat`, `vulnerability`, `risk`, and `control` compose, board-level discussions become parseable.

## Terms in this domain

- [[Asset]] — Anything of value worth protecting: data, systems, reputation, people.
- [[BCP and DRP]] — Business Continuity Plan keeps the business running; Disaster Recovery Plan restores IT systems.
- [[BIA]] — Business Impact Analysis.
- [[Control]] — A safeguard.
- [[Exploit]] — Code or technique that takes advantage of a vulnerability.
- [[GRC]] — Governance, Risk, and Compliance.
- [[Inherent Risk]] — Risk before any controls are applied.
- [[Policy Standard Procedure Guideline]] — A document hierarchy: policy (the what and why), standard (mandatory specifics), procedure (step-by-step how), guideline (recommendations).
- [[Residual Risk]] — Risk remaining after controls.
- [[Risk]] — Combination of likelihood and impact.
- [[Risk Appetite]] — How much risk leadership is willing to accept in pursuit of objectives.
- [[Risk Treatment]] — What you do with a risk: Accept, Mitigate (reduce), Transfer (e.g.
- [[RPO]] — Recovery Point Objective.
- [[RTO]] — Recovery Time Objective.
- [[Threat]] — A potential cause of harm to an asset.
- [[Threat Actor]] — The agent of a threat: script kiddie, cybercriminal, hacktivist, insider, nation-state, APT group.
- [[TPRM]] — Third-Party Risk Management.
- [[Vulnerability]] — A weakness that a threat could exploit.
