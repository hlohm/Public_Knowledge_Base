# MFA

> **Domain:** [[04 - Identity and Access Management|Identity & Access Management]]
> **Also known as:** 2FA, Multi-Factor Authentication, Two-Factor Authentication

**M**ulti-**F**actor **A**uthentication. Requiring two or more factors. **2FA** is the two-factor case.

**Context.** Two passwords aren't MFA — they must be different *types* of factor.
