# RPO

> **Domain:** [[02 - Risk and Governance|Risk & Governance]]

**R**ecovery **P**oint **O**bjective. Maximum acceptable data loss, measured in time (e.g. 15 minutes).

## See also

- [[RTO]]
- [[BCP and DRP]]

## Often confused with

- [[RTO]] — RPO = data loss tolerance; RTO = downtime tolerance.
