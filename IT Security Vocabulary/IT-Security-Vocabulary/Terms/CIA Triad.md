# CIA Triad

> **Domain:** [[01 - Core Principles|Core Principles & Models]]
> **Also known as:** CIA

**C**onfidentiality (only authorized parties can read), **I**ntegrity (data isn't tampered with), **A**vailability (system is reachable when needed). The foundational goals of security.

**Context.** Some add Authenticity and Non-repudiation to make the *Parkerian Hexad*. Whenever you analyze a control, ask which leg of CIA it protects.
