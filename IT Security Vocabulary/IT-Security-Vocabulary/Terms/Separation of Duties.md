# Separation of Duties

> **Domain:** [[01 - Core Principles|Core Principles & Models]]
> **Also known as:** SoD

Split sensitive actions across multiple people (e.g. developer ≠ deployer, requester ≠ approver). Prevents fraud and limits insider damage.
