# Risk Appetite

> **Domain:** [[02 - Risk and Governance|Risk & Governance]]

How much risk leadership is willing to accept in pursuit of objectives. Distinct from *risk tolerance*, which is the boundary you will not cross.

## See also

- [[Risk]]
- [[GRC]]

## Often confused with

- [[Risk]] — Appetite = what you want to take; tolerance = what you won't exceed; risk itself = likelihood × impact.
