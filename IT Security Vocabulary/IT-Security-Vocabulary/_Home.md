## How to use this vault

Each term lives in its own note under `Terms/`. Notes link to their related concepts with wikilinks (the `[[ ]]` syntax) — open any note and check the **Backlinks** pane (bottom-right) to see which other notes reference it. Over time the **Graph View** (Ctrl/Cmd-G) becomes the clearest picture of the field.

Don't read it cover-to-cover. Better path:

1. Pick a domain MOC (e.g. [[04 - Identity and Access Management]])
2. Read the intro and skim the term list
3. Open 3–4 terms you don't know — follow the *See also* links until you've satisfied your curiosity
4. Pop open the Graph View filtered to that domain — you'll see the cluster forming

Repeat for the next domain. Within a couple of weeks the vocabulary stops being foreign.

## Extending the vault

To add a new term, copy `Templates/Term Template.md` into `Terms/`, fill it in, and add wikilinks — backlinks happen automatically.

When you come across a new term in the wild (a Hacker News thread, a SOC ticket, a vendor blog), capture it here even with a one-line draft. Future you will be grateful.
