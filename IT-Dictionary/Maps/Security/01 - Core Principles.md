# Core Principles & Models

> These are the axioms of the field. Almost every other concept is justified by appealing to one of these — when you see a control or a design decision, ask which principle it serves.

## Terms in this domain

- [[AAA]] — Authentication, Authorization, Accounting.
- [[Attack Surface]] — The sum of all points where an attacker could try to enter or extract data.
- [[Blast Radius]] — How much damage one compromise can cause.
- [[CIA Triad]] — Confidentiality (only authorized parties can read), Integrity (data isn't tampered with), Availability (system is reachable when needed).
- [[Compartmentalization]] — Separating identities/infrastructure so one compromise doesn't expose the rest.
- [[Defense in Depth]] — Layer multiple independent controls so no single failure compromises the system.
- [[Fail Secure]] — When something breaks, default to denying access.
- [[Least Privilege]] — Give every user and process the minimum access required to do its job.
- [[Need to Know]] — Stricter cousin of least privilege focused on *information* rather than *permissions*.
- [[Non-repudiation]] — Inability of a party to deny having performed an action.
- [[Security through Obscurity]] — Relying on secrets *about the design* rather than secrets *within the design* (keys).

- [[Zero Trust]] — 'Never trust, always verify.' Treat every request as if it originated from an untrusted network, regardless of location.
