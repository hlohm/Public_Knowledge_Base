# Compliance & Standards

> Auditors speak these as a first language. You at least need a reading knowledge — and to know which framework to invoke in which situation.

## Terms in this domain

- [[Audit]] — Independent verification that controls are designed and operating as intended.
- [[BSI]] — Germany's federal cyber-security authority (Bundesamt für Sicherheit in der Informationstechnik).
- [[CCPA]] — California Consumer Privacy Act (amended by CPRA).
- [[CIS Benchmarks]] — Hardening guides for specific products (Windows, Linux, AWS, etc.).
- [[CIS Controls]] — Center for Internet Security's prioritized set of defensive actions.
- [[CMMC]] — Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification.
- [[COBIT]] — IT governance framework, complementary to ISO 27001 / ITIL.
- [[Data Classification]] — Labeling data by sensitivity (Public / Internal / Confidential / Restricted).
- [[DORA]] — Digital Operational Resilience Act.
- [[FedRAMP]] — US program standardizing security assessment for cloud services used by federal agencies.
- [[GDPR]] — General Data Protection Regulation.
- [[HIPAA]] — Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act.
- [[ISO 27001]] — International standard for an ISMS (Information Security Management System).
- [[IT-Grundschutz]] — The BSI's baseline methodology and control catalog for an ISMS.
- [[ITIL]] — IT service-management framework.
- [[NIS2]] — EU directive expanding cybersecurity obligations for critical and important entities.
- [[NIST CSF]] — Cybersecurity Framework.
- [[NIST SP 800-171]] — Controls for protecting CUI (Controlled Unclassified Information) in non-federal systems.
- [[NIST SP 800-53]] — US federal control catalog.
- [[PCI DSS]] — Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard.
- [[SoA]] — Statement of Applicability.
- [[SOC 1 and SOC 3]] — SOC 1 = controls relevant to financial reporting.
- [[SOC 2]] — AICPA audit report on a service org's controls against Trust Service Criteria (security, availability, confidentiality, processing integrity, privacy).
