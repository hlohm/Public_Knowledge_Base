The Email Ecosystem

Who the participants are, what they speak, and how a message is judged — transport layer and above

How to read this

Five views, each answering a different question. Path is the backbone — everything else is a detail lens on it. Click any box for the substance; nothing is explained on the diagram itself, so the picture stays legible.

Colour encodes role, not vendor: ■ client-side, ■ submission, ■ transfer, ■ delivery & storage, ■ DNS-backed, ■ policy & security, ■ other participants. Dashed edges are lookups and side-channels rather than the message itself.

The one idea to take away

Email has no single authority. Every property people expect from it — that the sender is who they claim, that the hop was encrypted, that the message is not spam — is a layer added later on top of a 1982 protocol that assumed a small network of trusted hosts. Almost everything in views 3 and 4 exists because SMTP itself verifies nothing. Read them as archaeology: each mechanism is a patch over a specific hole, and knowing which hole explains the design.
