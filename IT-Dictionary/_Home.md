## How this vault works

Every term lives in its own note under `Terms/`. Notes link to related concepts with `[[wikilinks]]`; open any note and check the **Backlinks** pane to see what points back. **Graph View** (Ctrl/Cmd-G) is the truest picture of the field.

Acronyms are the note titles (`TLS`, `RAII`); the spelled-out form lives in `aliases:` so a link to either resolves. A `de:` field carries the German term **only where Germans actually use a German word** — most IT jargon stays English even in German shops, so the field is often absent by design.

## Adding to it

See [[How to Extend]]. Short version: copy `Templates/Term Template.md` into `Terms/`, fill it in, wikilink generously. Backlinks happen automatically.
