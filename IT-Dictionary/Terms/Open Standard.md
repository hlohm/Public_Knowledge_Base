# Open Standard

> **Branch:** [[15 - Standards, Formats & Bodies|Standards, Formats & Bodies]]

A specification that is publicly available and free (or fairly) to implement, typically developed through an open, consensus process — HTTP, HTML, PDF, TCP/IP.

**Context.** The bedrock of interoperability and a hedge against lock-in: anyone can build a compatible implementation. The fight is usually at the edges — patent encumbrance, 'open' standards with hostile licensing, and embrace-extend-extinguish tactics that fork a standard into a proprietary dialect.
