# Enumeration

> **Domain:** [[12 - Offensive Security and Testing|Offensive Security & Testing]]

Active probing for services, users, shares, etc.

**Context.** The patient phase that decides everything after it — services, versions, users, shares, AD objects, cloud resources — because you exploit what you find, and thorough enumeration beats clever exploitation. It's also the noisiest phase, so on the defensive side it's a detection opportunity: bursts of failed logons, share access, LDAP queries, and port scans are enumeration leaving fingerprints.
