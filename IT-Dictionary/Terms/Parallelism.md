# Parallelism

> **Branch:** [[07 - Programming Languages|Programming Languages]]
> **German:** Parallelität

Actually executing multiple computations simultaneously, on multiple cores or machines — about *doing* many things at once for speed.

**Context.** Bounded by Amdahl's law: the serial fraction caps your speedup no matter how many cores you add. Requires concurrency as a precondition but is a distinct goal — concurrency is the structure, parallelism is the simultaneous execution.
