# Spear Phishing

> **Domain:** [[07 - Threats and Attacks|Threats & Attacks]]

Phishing targeted at a specific individual, usually after research.

**Context.** The research is the weapon: a message referencing your actual project, manager, or vendor defeats the "looks generic" instinct entirely. LinkedIn, breach data, and now AI make tailoring cheap at scale. Because content filtering struggles with a well-crafted one-off, the durable defenses are phishing-resistant MFA and out-of-band verification for anything that moves money or access.
