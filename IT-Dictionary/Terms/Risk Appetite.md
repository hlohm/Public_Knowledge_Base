# Risk Appetite

> **Domain:** [[02 - Risk and Governance|Risk & Governance]]

How much risk leadership is willing to accept in pursuit of objectives. Distinct from *risk tolerance*, which is the boundary you will not cross.

**Context.** Set by leadership, ideally in writing, because it's the standard against which security spending is justified. "We accept up to X days of outage for internal tools but none for the shop floor" is an appetite statement — and the reason two companies with identical tech make different patching decisions.
