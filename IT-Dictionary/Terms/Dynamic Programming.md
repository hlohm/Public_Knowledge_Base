# Dynamic Programming

> **Branch:** [[09 - Algorithms & Data Structures|Algorithms & Data Structures]]
> **Also known as:** DP

Solving a problem by breaking it into overlapping subproblems and storing their solutions (memoisation or tabulation) to avoid recomputation.

**Context.** Applies when a problem has optimal substructure *and* overlapping subproblems (the second is what separates it from plain divide-and-conquer). Turns exponential recursion into polynomial time — knapsack, edit distance, shortest paths. The hard part is always finding the right state and recurrence.
