# C2

> **Domain:** [[11 - SecOps Detection and Response|SecOps, Detection & Response]]
> **Also known as:** C&C, "Command and Control"

**C**ommand **a**nd **C**ontrol (also C&C). Attacker's channel for controlling compromised hosts.

**Context.** Cut the C2 and you cut the operator's hands off the keyboard — which is why egress control, protective DNS, and beaconing detection are such high-value defenses. Attackers fight back by hiding in legitimate channels: domain fronting, Slack/Telegram/Discord, cloud storage, and "living off trusted sites." The cat-and-mouse is largely about which outbound traffic you can afford to scrutinize.
