# Join

> **Branch:** [[06 - Data & Databases|Data & Databases]]

An operation combining rows from two or more tables on a related column — inner (matches only), left/right outer (keep unmatched on one side), cross (cartesian product).

**Context.** The relational model's superpower: store each fact once, recombine on demand. Join performance is why indexes on join keys matter, and the cost of joins at scale is a big part of why denormalisation and NoSQL exist.
