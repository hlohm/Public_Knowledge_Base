# Bandwidth

> **Branch:** [[04 - Networking|Networking]]
> **German:** Bandbreite

The maximum rate a link can carry data, e.g. 1 Gb/s — the width of the pipe, not the speed of a trip through it.

**Context.** Quoted in bits/s; divide by 8 for bytes/s. Achievable throughput is usually below this headline figure due to overhead and congestion.
