# ISO 8601

> **Branch:** [[01 - Computing Foundations|Computing Foundations]]

The international standard date/time format, e.g. 2026-06-01T14:30:00Z — year-first, zero-padded, with an explicit timezone offset.

**Context.** Its big-endian ordering means lexical sort equals chronological sort, which is why log files and filenames use it. Always store and exchange timestamps in this form to avoid the DD/MM vs MM/DD ambiguity.
