# BSI

> **Domain:** [[13 - Compliance and Standards|Compliance & Standards]]
> **Also known as:** Bundesamt für Sicherheit in der Informationstechnik, Federal Office for Information Security

**B**undesamt für **S**icherheit in der **I**nformationstechnik — Germany's federal cybersecurity authority. Publishes the IT-Grundschutz methodology, technical guidelines (TR), minimum standards, and the national situation reports; runs CERT-Bund.

**Context.** For German IT work the BSI is the reference point the way NIST is in the US: its Grundschutz catalogs drive public-sector and much enterprise security, its TLS and crypto technical guidelines (TR-02102) define 'state of the art' for compliance language, and BSI certification underpins everything from smart meters to ID cards. NIS2 implementation in Germany runs through BSI competence as well.
