# ZTNA

> **Domain:** [[10 - Cloud and Modern Architecture|Cloud & Modern Architecture]]
> **Also known as:** Zero Trust Network Access

**Z**ero **T**rust **N**etwork **A**ccess. App-level access without exposing the network. The modern replacement for client VPN.

**Context.** The operative difference from VPN: the user gets a brokered connection to *one application* after identity and device checks, not a network address with reach to everything routable — lateral movement has nothing to stand on, and the apps stay invisible from the internet. Per-app policies are also the migration cost: expect an application inventory project, not a box swap.
