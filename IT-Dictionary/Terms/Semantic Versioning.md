# Semantic Versioning

> **Branch:** [[08 - Software Engineering|Software Engineering]]
> **Also known as:** SemVer

A versioning convention `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`: bump MAJOR for breaking changes, MINOR for backward-compatible features, PATCH for backward-compatible fixes.

**Context.** A social contract so dependents know what an upgrade risks. Reality is messier — what counts as 'breaking' is fuzzy, and 'dependency hell' persists. SemVer paired with lockfiles is the practical compromise.
