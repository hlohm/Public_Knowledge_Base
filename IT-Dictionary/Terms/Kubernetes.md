# Kubernetes

> **Branch:** [[11 - Cloud & Infrastructure|Cloud & Infrastructure]]
> **Also known as:** K8s

An open-source container orchestration platform that automates deploying, scaling, healing, and networking containerised workloads across a cluster.

**Context.** The de facto standard, built on a declarative, reconciliation-loop model: you describe desired state, controllers continuously drive actual state toward it. Powerful and notoriously complex — often more than a given team needs, which fuels the recurring 'do we really need K8s?' debate.
