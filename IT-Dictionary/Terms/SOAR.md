# SOAR

> **Domain:** [[11 - SecOps Detection and Response|SecOps, Detection & Response]]
> **Also known as:** Security Orchestration Automation and Response

**S**ecurity **O**rchestration, **A**utomation, and **R**esponse. Workflow and automated response on top of SIEM data.

**Context.** Automates the tedious 80% of response — enrich the alert with threat intel, check the user, isolate the endpoint, open and route the ticket — so analysts spend time on judgment, not clicking. The trap is automating bad process: codify a workflow that works manually first. Increasingly folded into SIEM/XDR platforms rather than bought standalone.
