# Lossless Compression

> **Branch:** [[14 - Graphics, Media & HCI|Graphics, Media & HCI]]

Compression that reduces size with no loss of information — the original is reconstructed exactly (ZIP, PNG, FLAC, gzip).

**Context.** Mandatory when every bit matters (executables, text, archives, medical imaging) and bounded by entropy — you can't compress random or already-compressed data further. Works by removing statistical redundancy (repetition, predictable patterns) rather than perceptual detail.
