# TTPs

> **Domain:** [[11 - SecOps Detection and Response|SecOps, Detection & Response]]
> **Also known as:** Tactics Techniques and Procedures

**T**actics, **T**echniques, and **P**rocedures. How adversaries operate. The behavioral fingerprint at the top of the Pyramid of Pain.

**Context.** The behavioral fingerprint that survives tooling changes — an actor can swap malware and infrastructure, but *how* they operate (the techniques and their sequencing) is comparatively stable, which is why TTP-level detection is both the hardest to evade and the hardest to build. ATT&CK is essentially a structured catalog of TTPs; the Pyramid of Pain explains why they're worth the effort.
