# Tenant

> **Domain:** [[10 - Cloud and Modern Architecture|Cloud & Modern Architecture]]
> **Also known as:** Multi-tenancy

Logical isolation of customers sharing infrastructure. **Multi-tenancy** is the architecture.

**Context.** Tenant isolation is the load-bearing promise of every SaaS — and cross-tenant vulnerabilities are correspondingly the most severe cloud bug class. From the customer chair: know your tenant boundaries (an M365 tenant is an identity and data perimeter), and treat cross-tenant access features (guest users, B2B trust) as the deliberate holes they are.
