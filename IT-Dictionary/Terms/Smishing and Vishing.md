# Smishing and Vishing

> **Domain:** [[07 - Threats and Attacks|Threats & Attacks]]
> **Also known as:** Smishing, "Vishing"

SMS and voice variants of phishing.

**Context.** Channels with weaker filtering and higher implied trust than email — a text "from the bank" or a call "from IT support" lands with less scrutiny. The IT-helpdesk impersonation call (MFA-fatigue follow-up, or talking a user through approving a push) has driven real breaches, which is why help-desk identity verification is now a control worth writing down.
