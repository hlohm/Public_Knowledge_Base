# Watering Hole

> **Domain:** [[07 - Threats and Attacks|Threats & Attacks]]

Compromising a site the target frequents.

**Context.** When the target is too hardened to phish directly, compromise a site they're known to visit — an industry forum, a supplier's portal — and serve the exploit there. A hallmark of well-resourced actors going after a specific sector; cheaper for defenders to disrupt via patched browsers, isolation, and EDR than to predict which third-party site will be weaponized.
