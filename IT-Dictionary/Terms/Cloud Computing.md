# Cloud Computing

> **Branch:** [[11 - Cloud & Infrastructure|Cloud & Infrastructure]]
> **Also known as:** The Cloud

Renting computing as a metered utility: on-demand, self-service, elastic resources over the network, pooled across tenants. NIST's five characteristics remain the cleanest definition.

**Context.** The deep shift is economic and operational, not technical: capex→opex, capacity planning→elasticity, racking servers→calling APIs — which is why [[Infrastructure as Code]] and the [[Shared Responsibility Model]] are corollaries, not add-ons. 'The cloud is just someone else's computer' is true and misses the point: it's someone else's computer behind an API, with someone else's economies of scale.
