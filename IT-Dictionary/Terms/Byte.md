# Byte

> **Branch:** [[01 - Computing Foundations|Computing Foundations]]

A group of (almost always) 8 bits — the smallest individually addressable unit of memory on most machines. One byte holds 256 distinct values.

**Context.** The 8-bit byte is a convention, not a law; historic machines used 6, 7 or 9. The unambiguous term for 'exactly 8 bits' is **octet**, which is why RFCs say octet.
