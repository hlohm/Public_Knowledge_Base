# LLM

> **Branch:** [[13 - AI & Machine Learning|AI & Machine Learning]]
> **Also known as:** Large Language Model

A very large transformer trained on vast text to predict the next token, which yields broad language understanding and generation as an emergent result.

**Context.** The deceptively simple training objective — predict the next token — scales into apparent reasoning, translation, and coding once the model and data are large enough. Worth holding onto its nature: a powerful statistical model of text, prone to hallucination, not a database of facts or a reasoner in the human sense.
