# Coupling

> **Branch:** [[08 - Software Engineering|Software Engineering]]

The degree of interdependence between modules — how much one must know about another. Low (loose) coupling is the goal.

**Context.** The eternal pairing with cohesion: aim for loose coupling and high cohesion. Tight coupling is what makes a change in one place break things in five others. Most architectural patterns (interfaces, events, dependency injection) are coupling-reduction machinery.

## Often confused with

- [[Cohesion]] — Coupling is *between* modules (want it low); cohesion is *within* a module (want it high — one module, one clear job).
