# SOC 2

> **Domain:** [[13 - Compliance and Standards|Compliance & Standards]]

AICPA audit report on a service org's controls against **Trust Service Criteria** (security, availability, confidentiality, processing integrity, privacy). **Type I** = point in time; **Type II** = period of operating effectiveness.

**Context.** The de-facto entry ticket for selling SaaS to enterprises, especially in the US market. Always ask for Type II — Type I only proves the controls existed on one well-rehearsed day. Read the actual report, not the badge: scope, exceptions, and the auditor's opinion are where the truth lives.

## Often confused with

- [[ISO 27001]] — ISO = certification against a standard. SOC 2 = attestation by a CPA against trust criteria. Different audiences but overlapping control content.
