# Chain of Custody

> **Domain:** [[11 - SecOps Detection and Response|SecOps, Detection & Response]]

Documentation trail proving evidence integrity. Crucial for legal admissibility.

**Context.** If an incident might end in court, HR action, or insurance dispute, how you handled the evidence decides whether it's usable: documented who collected what, when, with which tool, and that it wasn't altered (hashes, write-blockers, working on copies). Get forensics/legal involved before pulling the plug — well-meaning cleanup destroys both evidence and the option to use it.
