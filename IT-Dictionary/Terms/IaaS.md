# IaaS

> **Branch:** [[11 - Cloud & Infrastructure|Cloud & Infrastructure]]
> **Also known as:** Infrastructure as a Service

Cloud service model providing raw virtualised infrastructure — compute, storage, networking — that you manage above the hypervisor (EC2, raw VMs).

**Context.** The bottom of the as-a-Service stack (IaaS → PaaS → SaaS), each layer handing more responsibility to the provider. IaaS gives the most control and the most operational burden; the trend is steadily up the stack toward managed and serverless.
