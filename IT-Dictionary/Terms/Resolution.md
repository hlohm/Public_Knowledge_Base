# Resolution

> **Branch:** [[14 - Graphics, Media & HCI|Graphics, Media & HCI]]
> **German:** Auflösung

The amount of detail in an image or display, given as pixel dimensions (1920×1080) or as a density (DPI/PPI).

**Context.** Two distinct meanings get conflated: pixel *count* (the grid size) and pixel *density* (how tightly packed, DPI/PPI). Density is what determines sharpness at a given size — the difference between print (300 DPI) and screen (~100 PPI), and why image assets ship at multiple densities.
