# FedRAMP

> **Domain:** [[13 - Compliance and Standards|Compliance & Standards]]

US program standardizing security assessment for cloud services used by federal agencies.

**Context.** Mostly relevant as a signal: a FedRAMP-authorized cloud service has survived a serious assessment against 800-53 controls. EU customers care indirectly — vendors often reuse the same hardened environments and evidence for other certifications.
