# Identity

> **Domain:** [[04 - Identity and Access Management|Identity & Access Management]]
> **German:** Identität

A digital representation of a user, service, or device.

**Context.** The inventory question "what identities exist?" now spans humans, service accounts, machine identities, API keys, and AI agents — and the non-human ones outnumber humans several times over in most environments. Each is an attack path; identity governance is asset management for this class.
