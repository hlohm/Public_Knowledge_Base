# KSPM

> **Domain:** [[10 - Cloud and Modern Architecture|Cloud & Modern Architecture]]
> **Also known as:** DSPM, "SSPM"

**K**ubernetes **S**ecurity **P**osture **M**anagement. Posture management applied to Kubernetes. Same family: **DSPM** (Data), **SSPM** (SaaS).

**Context.** The posture-management pattern applied per domain — the *SPM suffix family keeps growing (DSPM for data stores, SSPM for SaaS tenant configs). For Kubernetes specifically: RBAC sprawl, privileged pods, missing network policies, and exposed dashboards are the recurring findings. Conceptually all of them are "CSPM, but for X".
