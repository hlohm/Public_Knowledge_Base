# OSINT

> **Domain:** [[12 - Offensive Security and Testing|Offensive Security & Testing]]
> **Also known as:** Open Source Intelligence

**O**pen-**S**ource **I**ntelligence. Information gathering from public sources (LinkedIn, Shodan, DNS, GitHub).

**Context.** The reconnaissance that leaves no logs on the target: employee names and tech stack from LinkedIn, exposed services from Shodan, secrets and subdomains from GitHub and certificate transparency, breach data from past dumps. It powers spear phishing and scopes attacks — which is why the defensive counterpart is managing your own footprint: what does the internet already know about your org and people?
