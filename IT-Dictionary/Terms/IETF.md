# IETF

> **Branch:** [[15 - Standards, Formats & Bodies|Standards, Formats & Bodies]]
> **Also known as:** Internet Engineering Task Force

The open, volunteer standards body that develops and publishes the internet's core technical standards (as RFCs) — TCP/IP, HTTP, TLS, DNS.

**Context.** Famously informal and engineering-driven: no formal membership, decisions by 'rough consensus and running code' rather than votes. Its bottom-up, implementation-first culture is a deliberate contrast to the formal, treaty-style process of bodies like the ITU.
