# SSO

> **Domain:** [[04 - Identity and Access Management|Identity & Access Management]]
> **Also known as:** Single Sign-On

**S**ingle **S**ign-**O**n. Authenticate once, access many apps.

**Context.** SSO is not a protocol — it's an outcome achieved via SAML, OIDC, Kerberos, etc.
