# COBIT

> **Domain:** [[13 - Compliance and Standards|Compliance & Standards]]

IT governance framework, complementary to ISO 27001 / ITIL.

**Context.** ISACA's framework, strongest on governance: who decides, who is accountable, how IT value is measured. You'll meet it through auditors with ISACA certifications (CISA) more often than through engineering work. Think of it as the board-level lens, with ISO 27001 as the management system and CIS as the technical floor.
