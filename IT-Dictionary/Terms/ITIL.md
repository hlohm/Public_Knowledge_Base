# ITIL

> **Domain:** [[13 - Compliance and Standards|Compliance & Standards]]

IT service-management framework. Its change-management and incident-management processes pervade SOC operations.

**Context.** The vocabulary of every enterprise helpdesk: incident vs. problem vs. change, the CMDB, the service catalog. Security plugs into ITIL change management (emergency patches still need change records) and incident management (security incidents often arrive as ordinary tickets first).
