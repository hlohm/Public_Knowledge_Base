# IT-Grundschutz

> **Domain:** [[13 - Compliance and Standards|Compliance & Standards]]
> **Also known as:** BSI IT-Grundschutz, Grundschutz

The BSI's methodology and control catalog for building an ISMS: model your environment from standard building blocks (*Bausteine*), apply the cataloged requirements, and do explicit risk analysis only where the standard protection level isn't enough.

**Context.** Grundschutz is the pragmatic German answer to 'where do we start?' — instead of deriving every control from scratch via risk analysis (the pure ISO 27001 route), you inherit a vetted baseline per component type. Certification 'ISO 27001 auf der Basis von IT-Grundschutz' satisfies both worlds. The BSI standards 200-1/200-2/200-3 define ISMS, methodology, and risk analysis respectively; the Kompendium holds the building blocks.

## Often confused with

- [[ISO 27001]] — ISO 27001 specifies *what* an ISMS must achieve and leaves controls risk-driven; Grundschutz hands you a concrete catalog of *how*, with risk analysis as the exception.
