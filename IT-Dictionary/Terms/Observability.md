# Observability

> **Branch:** [[12 - DevOps & SRE|DevOps & SRE]]

The ability to understand a system's internal state from its external outputs — built on the three pillars: **metrics**, **logs**, and **traces**.

**Context.** The distinction from plain monitoring is the point: monitoring watches known failure modes (dashboards, alerts for things you anticipated); observability is about asking *new* questions of a live system to debug the unknown-unknowns inherent in distributed systems.
