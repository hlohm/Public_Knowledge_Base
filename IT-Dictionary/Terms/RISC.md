# RISC

> **Branch:** [[02 - Hardware & Architecture|Hardware & Architecture]]
> **Also known as:** Reduced Instruction Set Computer

A design philosophy favouring a small set of simple, fixed-length instructions that each do little but execute fast, leaning on the compiler to compose them.

**Context.** ARM and RISC-V are RISC; their efficiency is why they dominate phones and are climbing into laptops/servers. The RISC/CISC distinction has blurred — modern x86 chips decode their CISC instructions into RISC-like micro-ops internally.
