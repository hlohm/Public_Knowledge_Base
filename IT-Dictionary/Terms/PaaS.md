# PaaS

> **Branch:** [[11 - Cloud & Infrastructure|Cloud & Infrastructure]]
> **Also known as:** Platform as a Service

Cloud model providing a managed platform — runtime, OS, scaling, deploys — so you ship code without managing servers (Heroku, App Engine, Cloud Run).

**Context.** The middle layer: less control than IaaS, far less ops. The pitch is 'just push code'; the cost is the platform's constraints and lock-in. Blurs into serverless/FaaS at the lighter end.
