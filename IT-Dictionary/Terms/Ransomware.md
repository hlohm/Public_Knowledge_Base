# Ransomware

> **Domain:** [[07 - Threats and Attacks|Threats & Attacks]]
> **German:** Ransomware / Erpressungstrojaner

Encrypts data and demands payment. Modern variants also exfiltrate ('double extortion') and threaten to leak.

**Context.** Now an industry: ransomware-as-a-service, affiliates, initial-access brokers, and negotiation playbooks. Double (exfiltrate + encrypt) and triple (add DDoS/customer harassment) extortion mean backups alone no longer save you from the leak. The defenses that actually move the needle are offline/immutable backups, MFA on all remote access, fast patching of edge devices, and segmentation to limit spread.
