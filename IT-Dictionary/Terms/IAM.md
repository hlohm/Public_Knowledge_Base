# IAM

> **Domain:** [[04 - Identity and Access Management|Identity & Access Management]]
> **Also known as:** Identity and Access Management

**I**dentity and **A**ccess **M**anagement. The discipline of managing who can access what.

**Context.** The center of gravity of modern security: with perimeter dissolved, identity is the control point, and most breaches begin with a credential rather than an exploit. The lifecycle (joiner–mover–leaver) is the unglamorous core — orphaned accounts of departed employees are findable in nearly every audit.
