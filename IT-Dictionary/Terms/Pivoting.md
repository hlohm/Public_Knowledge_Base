# Pivoting

> **Domain:** [[12 - Offensive Security and Testing|Offensive Security & Testing]]

Using a compromised host as a launchpad to reach others.

**Context.** Turn the first compromised host into a relay — port forwarding, SOCKS proxies, tunneling — to reach segments you couldn't touch from outside. It's the practical mechanism behind lateral movement, and the reason flat networks are dangerous: one foothold becomes line-of-sight to everything. Segmentation and east-west monitoring are what make pivoting expensive.
