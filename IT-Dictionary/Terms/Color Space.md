# Color Space

> **Branch:** [[14 - Graphics, Media & HCI|Graphics, Media & HCI]]
> **Also known as:** Colour Space

A defined model and range (gamut) for representing colour numerically — sRGB, Display P3, Adobe RGB, CMYK for print.

**Context.** Why the same image looks different across devices, and why colour management exists. sRGB is the safe web default; wide-gamut spaces (P3) show more saturated colours on capable displays. The additive RGB (light/screens) vs subtractive CMYK (ink/print) distinction is the classic gotcha.
