# CIS Controls

> **Domain:** [[13 - Compliance and Standards|Compliance & Standards]]

Center for Internet Security's prioritized set of defensive actions.

**Context.** Where benchmarks harden one product, the Controls prioritize your whole program: 18 controls in implementation groups IG1–IG3, with IG1 as defensible minimum hygiene for any organization. Asset inventory is #1 and #2 for a reason — everything else depends on knowing what you have.
