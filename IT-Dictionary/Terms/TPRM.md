# TPRM

> **Domain:** [[02 - Risk and Governance|Risk & Governance]]

**T**hird-**P**arty **R**isk **M**anagement. Assessing risk introduced by vendors and partners. Hot since supply-chain attacks (SolarWinds, log4j, XZ).

**Context.** Your security is bounded by your vendors': the attacker who can't breach you directly will try your MSP, your software supplier, or your cleaning contractor's tablet. Practically this means security questionnaires, SOC 2 / ISO certificates as evidence, contract clauses on breach notification, and knowing which vendors hold your data.
