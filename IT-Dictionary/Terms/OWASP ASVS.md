# OWASP ASVS

> **Domain:** [[08 - Application Security|Application Security]]
> **Also known as:** Application Security Verification Standard

**A**pplication **S**ecurity **V**erification **S**tandard. Detailed checklist for verifying app security.

**Context.** Where the Top 10 raises awareness, ASVS gives you testable requirements at three levels — usable as a security backlog, a pentest scope, or acceptance criteria in contracts. Pick the level that matches the app's risk (L1 baseline, L2 for most business apps, L3 for high-assurance) rather than treating all 250+ controls as mandatory.
