# DevOps

> **Branch:** [[12 - DevOps & SRE|DevOps & SRE]]

A culture and set of practices uniting software development and operations to shorten the delivery cycle through automation, shared ownership, and fast feedback.

**Context.** Fundamentally cultural before it's tooling — 'you build it, you run it' dissolving the dev-vs-ops wall. The DORA metrics (deploy frequency, lead time, change-fail rate, time-to-restore) are the evidence-based way to measure whether it's actually working.
