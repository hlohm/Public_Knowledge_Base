# Dwell Time

> **Domain:** [[11 - SecOps Detection and Response|SecOps, Detection & Response]]

Duration attacker was present before detection. Shrinking this is the point of most SOC investment.

**Context.** The number that captures how badly detection failed: industry medians fell from many months to a few weeks, but ransomware crews now compress their own dwell to days or hours, so the target keeps moving. Every investment in EDR, hunting, and tuned detections is ultimately an argument about shrinking this — the longer the dwell, the deeper the cleanup.
