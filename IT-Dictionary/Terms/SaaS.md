# SaaS

> **Branch:** [[11 - Cloud & Infrastructure|Cloud & Infrastructure]]
> **Also known as:** Software as a Service

Cloud model delivering finished software over the web on subscription — the provider runs everything; you just use it (Gmail, Salesforce, Slack).

**Context.** The top of the stack and the dominant commercial software model. From the user's side it's just 'an app in the browser'; the engineering interest is the multi-tenancy underneath — one codebase and infrastructure serving many isolated customers.
