# Commit

> **Branch:** [[08 - Software Engineering|Software Engineering]]

A recorded snapshot of changes with a message, author, timestamp, and pointer to its parent(s) — the atomic unit of history.

**Context.** Good commit hygiene (small, focused, well-described, each leaving the build green) is what makes history a usable tool rather than noise. In Git a commit is identified by the hash of its content, which is why history is tamper-evident.
