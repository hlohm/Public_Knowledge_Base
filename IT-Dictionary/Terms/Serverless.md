# Serverless

> **Branch:** [[11 - Cloud & Infrastructure|Cloud & Infrastructure]]

An execution model where the provider fully manages server allocation and scaling, including scaling to zero — you reason about code and events, not machines.

**Context.** There are still servers, of course; you just don't manage or pay for idle ones. Encompasses FaaS plus managed databases, queues, and storage. The tradeoff: minimal ops and pay-per-use vs. cold starts, vendor lock-in, and harder local testing.
