# NIST

> **Branch:** [[15 - Standards, Formats & Bodies|Standards, Formats & Bodies]]
> **Also known as:** National Institute of Standards and Technology

A US agency whose standards carry global weight in computing security — the AES and SHA cryptographic standards, the post-quantum crypto selections, and the Cybersecurity Framework.

**Context.** The one most entangled with your security work: NIST runs the open competitions that pick worldwide crypto standards (AES, SHA-3, the post-quantum algorithms), and SP 800-series / the CSF are reference points for security programmes well beyond the US government.
