# Botnet

> **Domain:** [[07 - Threats and Attacks|Threats & Attacks]]

Network of compromised hosts ('zombies') under attacker control.

**Context.** The rentable engine behind DDoS, spam, credential stuffing, and crypto-mining — increasingly built from IoT devices with default passwords (Mirai). From the defender's side the tell is outbound: a host beaconing to C2 on a schedule, which is why egress monitoring and protective DNS catch bots that endpoint AV missed.
