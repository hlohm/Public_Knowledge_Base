# Non-repudiation

> **Domain:** [[01 - Core Principles|Core Principles & Models]]

Inability of a party to deny having performed an action. Achieved through digital signatures and audit logging.

**Context.** Matters whenever actions have legal or financial weight: signed contracts, change approvals, financial transactions, audit trails. Technically it requires that the signing key is provably under one person's sole control — which is why qualified signatures (eIDAS) involve smartcards or HSMs, and why shared admin accounts destroy non-repudiation entirely.
