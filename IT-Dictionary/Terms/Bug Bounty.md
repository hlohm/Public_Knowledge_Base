# Bug Bounty

> **Domain:** [[09 - Vulnerabilities and Exposure|Vulnerabilities & Exposure]]

Program paying external researchers for valid vulnerability reports.

**Context.** Crowdsourced testing that pays for results, run on platforms (HackerOne, Bugcrowd) or independently. The prerequisite everyone skips: a bounty program without a mature internal triage-and-fix process just generates reports you can't action and frustrated researchers who go public. Start with a VDP (a safe-harbor reporting channel, no payouts) and earn your way to bounties.
