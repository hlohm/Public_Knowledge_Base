# SOLID

> **Branch:** [[08 - Software Engineering|Software Engineering]]

Five OOP design principles: **S**ingle responsibility, **O**pen/closed, **L**iskov substitution, **I**nterface segregation, **D**ependency inversion.

**Context.** A useful checklist for spotting brittle designs, though dogmatic application produces its own over-abstracted mess. Dependency inversion (depend on abstractions, not concretions) and single responsibility are the two that pay off most often.
