# Threat Actor

> **Domain:** [[02 - Risk and Governance|Risk & Governance]]

The agent of a threat: script kiddie, cybercriminal, hacktivist, insider, nation-state, APT group.

**Context.** The category predicts behavior: criminals optimize for money (ransomware, BEC) and leave when costs rise; nation-states optimize for access and persist for years; insiders bypass the perimeter entirely. Defenses should match your realistic adversaries — most organizations face criminals, not the NSA.
