# CIS Benchmarks

> **Domain:** [[13 - Compliance and Standards|Compliance & Standards]]

Hardening guides for specific products (Windows, Linux, AWS, etc.).

**Context.** The pragmatic answer to "how do I harden this?" — free PDFs with per-setting rationale and audit steps for most OSes, browsers, and cloud platforms. Level 1 is the sane baseline; Level 2 trades usability for defense-in-depth. Tooling (CIS-CAT, many vulnerability scanners) can score compliance automatically.
