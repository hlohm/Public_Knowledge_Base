# Transformer

> **Branch:** [[13 - AI & Machine Learning|AI & Machine Learning]]

The neural architecture (2017, 'Attention Is All You Need') built on self-attention, which processes all positions in a sequence in parallel and weighs their relationships.

**Context.** The architecture behind essentially every modern large language model. Its win over RNNs was twofold: parallel processing (so you can train on massive data fast) and attention's ability to relate distant tokens directly. Scaling it up is what produced GPT and the LLM era.
