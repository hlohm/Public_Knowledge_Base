# Moore's Law

> **Branch:** [[02 - Hardware & Architecture|Hardware & Architecture]]

The observation (Gordon Moore, 1965) that the number of transistors on a chip roughly doubles every ~two years. An economic trend, not a physical law.

**Context.** It held for decades but is slowing as features approach atomic scale. Crucially, *single-thread* speed gains stalled around 2005 (the end of Dennard scaling), which is why the industry pivoted to multi-core — and why concurrency became every programmer's problem.
