# P vs NP

> **Branch:** [[10 - Theory of Computation|Theory of Computation]]

The open question of whether every problem whose solution can be *verified* quickly (NP) can also be *solved* quickly (P) — i.e. does P = NP?

**Context.** The most famous open problem in computer science (a Clay Millennium Prize). Almost everyone believes P ≠ NP. The stakes are enormous: if P = NP, most modern cryptography would collapse and optimisation would transform. Until then, NP-hardness is the practical signal to stop seeking an exact polynomial algorithm and use heuristics.
