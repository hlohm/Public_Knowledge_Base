# Accessibility

> **Branch:** [[14 - Graphics, Media & HCI|Graphics, Media & HCI]]
> **Also known as:** a11y
> **German:** Barrierefreiheit

Designing software usable by people with disabilities — visual, motor, auditory, cognitive — via screen-reader support, keyboard navigation, sufficient contrast, captions, and more.

**Context.** Both an ethical baseline and, increasingly, a legal requirement (EU Accessibility Act, ADA, WCAG conformance levels). Often deferred and expensive to retrofit, cheap to build in. The reliable payoff: accessible design tends to be better, clearer design for everyone (curb-cut effect).
