# Audit

> **Domain:** [[13 - Compliance and Standards|Compliance & Standards]]
> **German:** Audit / Prüfung

Independent verification that controls are designed and operating as intended.

**Context.** Audits run on evidence: screenshots, exported configs, log samples, signed approvals. The operational trick is to generate evidence as a byproduct of normal work (tickets, change records) instead of reconstructing it in a panic the week before. Internal audits exist to find the gaps before the external auditor bills for finding them.
