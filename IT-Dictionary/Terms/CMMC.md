# CMMC

> **Domain:** [[13 - Compliance and Standards|Compliance & Standards]]
> **Also known as:** Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification

**C**ybersecurity **M**aturity **M**odel **C**ertification. US DoD program building on 800-171.

**Context.** Relevant if you sell into the US defense supply chain, directly or as a sub-supplier. Three levels; Level 2 maps to NIST 800-171 and requires third-party assessment. The teeth: no certification, no contract.
