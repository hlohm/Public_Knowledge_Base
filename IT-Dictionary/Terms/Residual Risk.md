# Residual Risk

> **Domain:** [[02 - Risk and Governance|Risk & Governance]]

Risk remaining after controls. The one you actually live with.

**Context.** The decision-grade number. Someone with authority must formally accept residual risk — in writing — which is the entire point of a risk register: it moves the decision from the admin who noticed the problem to the management that owns it.
