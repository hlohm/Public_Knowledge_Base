# Ethernet

> **Branch:** [[04 - Networking|Networking]]
> **Also known as:** IEEE 802.3

The dominant wired LAN technology (IEEE 802.3): frames addressed by MAC, carried today over twisted pair or fiber at 1/10/25/100+ Gb/s. Wi-Fi (802.11) deliberately presents itself to higher layers as Ethernet.

**Context.** Ethernet won by evolving: the shared-coax CSMA/CD origins are extinct — modern Ethernet is point-to-point links into switches, full duplex, no collisions. What survives is the frame format and the economics. Practical layer: auto-negotiation mismatches, the 1500-byte default MTU (and jumbo frames), and PoE turning the same cable into a power grid for phones, APs, and cameras.
