# Warehouse-Scale Computer

> **Branch:** [[11 - Cloud & Infrastructure|Cloud & Infrastructure]]
> **Also known as:** WSC

The view of an entire datacenter as a single computer — the unit that is designed, provisioned and optimised as a whole — rather than as a pile of servers that merely share a building.

**Context.** Coined in Barroso & Hölzle's work at Google. The argument: at hyperscale the meaningful machine is the facility, with power, cooling, network fabric and software co-designed. A useful counterpoint to the hardware view that "one computer" stops at the coherence domain — both are right, for different purposes.
