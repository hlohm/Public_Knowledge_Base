# SoA

> **Domain:** [[13 - Compliance and Standards|Compliance & Standards]]
> **Also known as:** Statement of Applicability

**S**tatement **o**f **A**pplicability. ISO 27001 document listing which controls apply and why.

**Context.** The ISO 27001 audit's table of contents: all 93 Annex A controls, each marked applicable or excluded *with justification*. Excluding a control is fine; failing to justify the exclusion is a finding. Auditors read it first and sample against it.
