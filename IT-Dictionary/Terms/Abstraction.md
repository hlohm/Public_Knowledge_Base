# Abstraction

> **Branch:** [[01 - Computing Foundations|Computing Foundations]]

Hiding detail behind a simpler interface so you can reason about a system without holding all of it in your head at once. The central tool of all software design.

**Context.** Every layer in computing is an abstraction over the one below — sockets over packets, files over disk blocks, variables over memory. 'Leaky abstractions' (Spolsky) are ones whose hidden details still force their way through.
