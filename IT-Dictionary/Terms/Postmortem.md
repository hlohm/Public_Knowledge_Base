# Postmortem

> **Branch:** [[12 - DevOps & SRE|DevOps & SRE]]
> **Also known as:** Incident Retrospective, Post-Mortem, After-Action Review

A written analysis after an incident — timeline, impact, root causes, and corrective actions — ideally **blameless**, focused on systemic fixes rather than fault.

**Context.** The blameless framing is the load-bearing part: people share what really happened only when they won't be punished for it, and almost every incident has systemic causes (the human 'error' was the last link in a chain of bad defaults). The output is action items that prevent recurrence. The same artifact appears in security incident response as an *after-action review* — same blameless principle, applied to a breach rather than an outage.
