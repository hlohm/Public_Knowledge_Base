# Twelve-factor App

> **Branch:** [[12 - DevOps & SRE|DevOps & SRE]]
> **Also known as:** 12-Factor App

A methodology of twelve principles for building cloud-native apps — config in the environment, stateless processes, explicit dependencies, logs as event streams, and so on.

**Context.** The implicit blueprint behind most PaaS and container platforms. The principles that matter most in practice: strict config/code separation (no secrets in code) and statelessness (so you can scale horizontally and treat instances as disposable).
