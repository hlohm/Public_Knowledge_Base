# Data Classification

> **Domain:** [[13 - Compliance and Standards|Compliance & Standards]]
> **German:** Datenklassifizierung

Labeling data by sensitivity (Public / Internal / Confidential / Restricted). Drives handling rules.

**Context.** Classification is the prerequisite that makes other controls decidable: DLP rules, encryption requirements, retention, and who may email what to whom all key off the label. Keep the scheme to 3–4 levels — schemes with seven levels get ignored. In M365, sensitivity labels are the implementation.
