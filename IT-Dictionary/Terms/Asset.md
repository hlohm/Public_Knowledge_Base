# Asset

> **Domain:** [[02 - Risk and Governance|Risk & Governance]]
> **German:** Asset / Wert

Anything of value worth protecting: data, systems, reputation, people. The starting point of any risk assessment.

**Context.** You can't protect what you haven't inventoried — which is why asset management is CIS Control #1 and the most commonly failed audit item. Don't forget the non-obvious assets: domain names, TLS certificates, API keys, SaaS tenants, and the institutional knowledge in one admin's head.
