# MTTD and MTTR

> **Domain:** [[11 - SecOps Detection and Response|SecOps, Detection & Response]]
> **Also known as:** MTTD, "MTTR", "Mean Time to Detect", "Mean Time to Respond"

**M**ean **T**ime to **D**etect / **R**espond. A SOC's headline metrics.

**Context.** Headline metrics that are easy to game — narrow the scope and the numbers improve while security doesn't — so treat them as trend lines for your own program, not cross-org benchmarks. The pair tells a story together: fast detection with slow response still means a long intrusion. Pair with dwell time and tie improvements to specific process or tooling changes.
