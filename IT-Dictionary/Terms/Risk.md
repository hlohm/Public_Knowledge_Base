# Risk

> **Domain:** [[02 - Risk and Governance|Risk & Governance]]
> **German:** Risiko

Combination of likelihood and impact. Roughly: *Risk = Threat × Vulnerability × Impact.*

**Context.** The formula is a model, not math — the multiplication is conceptual. Qualitative scoring (likelihood × impact matrices) dominates in practice because honest probability estimates are rare. The discipline it enforces is what matters: stop treating every finding as equally urgent.
