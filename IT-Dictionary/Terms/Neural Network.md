# Neural Network

> **Branch:** [[13 - AI & Machine Learning|AI & Machine Learning]]
> **Also known as:** Artificial Neural Network, ANN
> **German:** neuronales Netz

A model of interconnected layers of simple units ('neurons') whose weighted connections are tuned during training — a universal function approximator loosely inspired by biology.

**Context.** The 'loosely inspired by the brain' framing oversells the biology; mechanically it's stacked matrix multiplications with non-linear activations between them. The non-linearity is essential — without it, any depth collapses to a single linear layer. Depth is what 'deep' learning adds.
