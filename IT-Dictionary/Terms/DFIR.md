# DFIR

> **Domain:** [[11 - SecOps Detection and Response|SecOps, Detection & Response]]
> **Also known as:** Digital Forensics and Incident Response

**D**igital **F**orensics and **I**ncident **R**esponse. Combines evidence collection with response.

**Context.** Where investigation meets the clock: forensics wants to preserve and analyze carefully, response wants to contain *now*, and IR is the art of doing both without ruining either. Core skills span disk and memory forensics, log timeline analysis, and (increasingly) cloud and EDR telemetry. Have the runbook, contacts, and tooling ready before the incident — DFIR improvised at 2am goes badly.
