# WASM

> **Branch:** [[05 - Internet & Web|Internet & Web]]
> **Also known as:** WebAssembly

A portable binary instruction format that runs in browsers (and beyond) at near-native speed, as a compilation target for C, Rust, Go, and more.

**Context.** Lets non-JavaScript languages run on the web in a sandbox, and is increasingly used server-side and at the edge as a lightweight, secure alternative to containers.
