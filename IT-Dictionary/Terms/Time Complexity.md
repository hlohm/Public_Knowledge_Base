# Time Complexity

> **Branch:** [[09 - Algorithms & Data Structures|Algorithms & Data Structures]]

How an algorithm's running time scales with input size, expressed in Big-O — O(1), O(log n), O(n), O(n log n), O(n²), O(2ⁿ)…

**Context.** The hierarchy to internalise: constant and log are great, linear and linearithmic are fine, quadratic hurts at scale, exponential is intractable past tiny inputs. Worst-case is the usual quote; average and amortised matter too (hash tables, dynamic arrays).
