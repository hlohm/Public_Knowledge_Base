# N-Day

> **Domain:** [[07 - Threats and Attacks|Threats & Attacks]]

Known and patched vuln still being exploited against unpatched systems.

**Context.** The unglamorous majority of real-world compromise: the patch exists, the exploit is public, and the target hadn't applied it. The window between Patch Tuesday and Exploit Wednesday is measured in hours for some bugs now. KEV exists precisely to flag the N-days under active exploitation — patch those on a deadline, not a backlog.
