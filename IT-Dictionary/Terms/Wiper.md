# Wiper

> **Domain:** [[07 - Threats and Attacks|Threats & Attacks]]

Pretends to be ransomware but destroys data with no recovery option. Often used in nation-state attacks.

**Context.** Looks like ransomware, but there is no key and no recovery — destruction is the goal, sometimes wearing a ransom note as misdirection (NotPetya). The weapon of geopolitical conflict (Ukraine, Shamoon at Saudi Aramco). The defensive lesson is blunt: offline, tested backups are the only real answer, because paying buys nothing.
