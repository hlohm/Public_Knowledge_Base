# IEEE

> **Branch:** [[15 - Standards, Formats & Bodies|Standards, Formats & Bodies]]
> **Also known as:** Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers

A major professional body and standards organisation behind foundational technical standards — Ethernet (802.3), Wi-Fi (802.11), and floating-point arithmetic (754).

**Context.** Its 802 family standardised the LAN/wireless world you work in daily; IEEE 754 is why floating-point behaves identically across hardware. Also a huge publisher of research. Pronounced 'I-triple-E'.
