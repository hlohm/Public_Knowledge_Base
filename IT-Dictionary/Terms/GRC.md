# GRC

> **Domain:** [[02 - Risk and Governance|Risk & Governance]]

**G**overnance, **R**isk, and **C**ompliance. Umbrella discipline wrapping strategy, risk management, and regulatory adherence.

**Context.** GRC is where security meets paperwork, and it pays the bills: certifications win contracts, and regulators fine their absence. Tooling (vanta-style platforms, ServiceNow GRC) automates evidence collection. The risk for technical people is treating GRC as theater — done well, it forces the inventory and review discipline that pure tech work skips.
