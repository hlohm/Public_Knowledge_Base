# Patch Management

> **Domain:** [[06 - Endpoint and Host Security|Endpoint & Host Security]]
> **German:** Patch-Management

Process of testing and deploying vendor fixes. The unsexy practice that prevents most breaches.

**Context.** The process beats the heroics: defined rings (test group → broad rollout), a deadline policy tied to severity (KEV/critical: days, not months), and coverage that includes the unloved third tier — browsers, Java, VPN appliances, firmware, hypervisors. Measure time-to-patch honestly; the gap between patch release and exploitation keeps shrinking.
