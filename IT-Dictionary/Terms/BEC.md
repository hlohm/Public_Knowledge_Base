# BEC

> **Domain:** [[07 - Threats and Attacks|Threats & Attacks]]
> **Also known as:** Business Email Compromise

**B**usiness **E**mail **C**ompromise. Impersonating execs/vendors to redirect payments. Massive financial-loss category.

**Context.** Consistently among the costliest categories by dollar loss — no malware, just a convincing email that reroutes an invoice payment or triggers a wire. Defenses are as much process as technical: out-of-band verification for payment changes, strict email authentication (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) against spoofing, and alerting on inbox rules that auto-hide the attacker's replies.
