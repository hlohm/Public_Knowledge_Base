# Penetration Test

> **Domain:** [[09 - Vulnerabilities and Exposure|Vulnerabilities & Exposure]]
> **Also known as:** Pentest
> **German:** Penetrationstest

Authorized simulated attack to find exploitable weaknesses. Types: black/gray/white box.

**Context.** A point-in-time, scoped assessment — valuable, but commonly oversold and misunderstood: a clean pentest report means "these testers didn't find more in this scope and timebox," not "you're secure." Get the *findings and methodology*, not just the executive summary; a checklist-driven "pentest" that's really an automated scan with a logo is the thing to avoid. Distinct from a red team (goal-driven, stealthy, tests detection).
