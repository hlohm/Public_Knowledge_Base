# Cohesion

> **Branch:** [[08 - Software Engineering|Software Engineering]]

How focused a single module is — high cohesion means its parts all serve one well-defined purpose.

**Context.** High cohesion makes code easy to name, find, and change; low cohesion is the 'utils.js with 40 unrelated functions' smell. Together with loose coupling, it's the two-word summary of good modular design.
