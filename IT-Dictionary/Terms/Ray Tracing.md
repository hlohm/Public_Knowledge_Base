# Ray Tracing

> **Branch:** [[14 - Graphics, Media & HCI|Graphics, Media & HCI]]

A rendering technique that simulates light by tracing the paths of rays through a scene, producing physically accurate reflections, refractions, and shadows.

**Context.** Long the gold standard for offline/film rendering because it models light realistically; the recent shift is dedicated GPU hardware (RTX) bringing it toward real time, usually hybridised with rasterisation and AI denoising/upscaling to hit playable frame rates.
