# Firmware

> **Branch:** [[02 - Hardware & Architecture|Hardware & Architecture]]

Low-level software stored on a device that controls its hardware directly — sitting between pure hardware and the operating system. BIOS/UEFI is firmware.

**Context.** Because it runs before and beneath the OS, compromised firmware is extremely hard to detect or remove — a key reason for Secure Boot and hardware roots of trust. You also can't easily 'reinstall the OS' your way out of it.
