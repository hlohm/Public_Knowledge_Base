# Inherent Risk

> **Domain:** [[02 - Risk and Governance|Risk & Governance]]

Risk before any controls are applied.

**Context.** A useful fiction — no real system has zero controls — but it anchors the comparison: inherent risk minus control effect equals residual risk. Auditors use the gap to judge whether your controls actually matter.

## Often confused with

- [[Residual Risk]] — Inherent = before controls; residual = what's left after.
