# HIPAA

> **Domain:** [[13 - Compliance and Standards|Compliance & Standards]]
> **Also known as:** PHI, "Protected Health Information"

**H**ealth **I**nsurance **P**ortability and **A**ccountability **A**ct. US law protecting **PHI** (Protected Health Information).

**Context.** US-specific, but the pattern recurs in every health-data regime: a defined data class (PHI), a Security Rule of required safeguards, and contracts (Business Associate Agreements) binding vendors. German equivalents live in §203 StGB, SGB, and state hospital laws — stricter, not looser.
