# NAT

> **Branch:** [[04 - Networking|Networking]]
> **Also known as:** Network Address Translation

Rewriting IP addresses/ports at a boundary so many private hosts share one public address.

**Context.** Bought IPv4 a couple of decades by hiding whole networks behind one address; the cost is broken end-to-end addressing and the NAT-traversal dance (STUN/TURN).
