# CDN

> **Branch:** [[05 - Internet & Web|Internet & Web]]
> **Also known as:** Content Delivery Network

A geographically distributed network of caching servers that serve content from a location near each user, cutting latency and origin load.

**Context.** Wins on the physics of latency by moving bytes closer. Also a front-line DDoS absorber and TLS terminator. Cache invalidation (purging stale content) is the perennial headache.
