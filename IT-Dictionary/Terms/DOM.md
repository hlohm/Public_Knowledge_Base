# DOM

> **Branch:** [[05 - Internet & Web|Internet & Web]]
> **Also known as:** Document Object Model

The browser's in-memory tree representation of an HTML page that scripts can read and modify to change what's displayed.

**Context.** Direct DOM manipulation is slow, which is why frameworks (React) diff a 'virtual DOM' and batch real updates. XSS is fundamentally an attacker writing into your DOM.
