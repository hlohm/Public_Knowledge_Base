# BCP and DRP

> **Domain:** [[02 - Risk and Governance|Risk & Governance]]
> **Also known as:** BCP, "DRP", "Business Continuity Plan", "Disaster Recovery Plan"

**B**usiness **C**ontinuity **P**lan keeps the business running; **D**isaster **R**ecovery **P**lan restores IT systems. BCP is broader.

**Context.** The DR plan that has never been tested is a work of fiction. Restore drills, documented runbooks reachable *without* the systems they describe, and offline copies of the plan itself are the difference between an incident and an extinction event. Ransomware made this a security topic, not just an ops one.
