# Backward Compatibility

> **Branch:** [[15 - Standards, Formats & Bodies|Standards, Formats & Bodies]]
> **German:** Abwärtskompatibilität

The property that newer versions of a system, format, or interface continue to work with inputs, data, or clients built for older versions.

**Context.** The constraint that quietly shapes long-lived systems — it's why x86 still carries 1970s baggage and the web never breaks old pages ('don't break userspace', as Linus puts it). The discipline that builds trust and the burden that accretes cruft; SemVer exists precisely to signal when it's broken.
