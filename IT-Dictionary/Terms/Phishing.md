# Phishing

> **Domain:** [[07 - Threats and Attacks|Threats & Attacks]]

Social engineering via message to trick users into clicking, opening, or revealing.

**Context.** Still the number-one initial access vector, and it has evolved past "spot the typo": AitM kits defeat ordinary MFA, QR codes (quishing) dodge URL scanners, and AI makes the lures fluent. Layered defense — DMARC, link/attachment detonation, FIDO2 for the accounts that matter, and reporting buttons that feed the SOC — beats any single control, including awareness training.
