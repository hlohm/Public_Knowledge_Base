# Blue Team

> **Domain:** [[12 - Offensive Security and Testing|Offensive Security & Testing]]

The defenders — the people and function responsible for detecting, responding to, and hardening against attacks. Counterpart to the Red Team.

**Context.** The unfair fight made explicit: the blue team must close every gap, the red team needs one. That asymmetry is the argument for defense-in-depth, good telemetry, and purple-teaming — collaborating with offense to turn each successful attack into a new detection rather than a scoreboard loss. Most security work is blue-team work, even when it's not called that.
