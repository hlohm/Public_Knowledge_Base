# NIST SP 800-53

> **Domain:** [[13 - Compliance and Standards|Compliance & Standards]]
> **Also known as:** SP 800-53

US federal control catalog. Comprehensive; influence beyond government.

**Context.** The encyclopedic reference: ~1000 controls with enhancements, organized in families (AC, AU, IR, SC…). Nobody implements all of it — you select a baseline (low/moderate/high) and tailor. Useful to a practitioner as the place where any conceivable control is already named, defined, and numbered.
