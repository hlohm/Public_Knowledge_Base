# JavaScript

> **Branch:** [[07 - Programming Languages|Programming Languages]]
> **Also known as:** JS, ECMAScript

The language of the web, standardized as **ECMAScript**: dynamically typed, prototype-based, single-threaded with an event loop — and via Node.js, V8, and WASM hosts, long since escaped the browser.

**Context.** JavaScript's design is its history: ten days of prototyping in 1995, then thirty years of backward-compatible evolution — which explains both the famous warts (`==` coercion, `this` binding) and the modern fixes layered on top (`===`, `let/const`, modules, async/await over the callback era). TypeScript's rise is the ecosystem voting that gradual [[Static Typing]] was the missing piece.
