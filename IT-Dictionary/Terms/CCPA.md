# CCPA

> **Domain:** [[13 - Compliance and Standards|Compliance & Standards]]
> **Also known as:** CPRA

California Consumer Privacy Act (amended by **CPRA**). California's data-privacy laws.

**Context.** Matters to EU-based teams mainly when serving California consumers. Conceptually GDPR-lite: disclosure, deletion, and opt-out-of-sale rights, but no general consent requirement. CPRA added a dedicated enforcement agency.
