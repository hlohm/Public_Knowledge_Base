# Risk Treatment

> **Domain:** [[02 - Risk and Governance|Risk & Governance]]

What you do with a risk: **Accept**, **Mitigate** (reduce), **Transfer** (e.g. insurance), or **Avoid** (don't do the thing).

**Context.** Mitigate is the default reflex, but the other three are legitimate: cyber insurance transfers, discontinuing a risky legacy service avoids, and a signed acceptance of a low-impact risk is often the rational call. Untracked, silent acceptance — the risk nobody wrote down — is the only invalid option.
