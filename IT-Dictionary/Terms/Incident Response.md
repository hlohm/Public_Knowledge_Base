# Incident Response

> **Domain:** [[11 - SecOps Detection and Response|SecOps, Detection & Response]]
> **Also known as:** IR

Coordinated action to contain and recover from an incident. NIST phases: Prepare → Detect & Analyze → Contain → Eradicate → Recover → Post-incident.

**Context.** The plan is for when judgment is hardest — 3am, partial information, pressure to "just fix it." The recurring real-world failures aren't technical: no clear authority to disconnect production, contact lists out of date, no out-of-band comms when email/Teams is compromised, and containment that tips off the attacker. Tabletop the plan; the first time you run it shouldn't be live.
