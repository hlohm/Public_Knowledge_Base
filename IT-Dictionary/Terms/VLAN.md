# VLAN

> **Domain:** [[05 - Network Security|Network Security]]
> **German:** VLAN

**V**irtual **L**ocal **A**rea **N**etwork. Logical network segment on shared physical infrastructure.

**Context.** The everyday segmentation tool, with everyday failure modes: VLAN 1 left as a kitchen-sink default, trunk ports carrying everything everywhere, and the misconception that VLANs alone are a security boundary — without inter-VLAN ACLs/firewalling, they only separate broadcast domains. Hopping attacks are real but rare; misconfiguration is the practical risk.
