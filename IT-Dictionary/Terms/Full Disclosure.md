# Full Disclosure

> **Domain:** [[09 - Vulnerabilities and Exposure|Vulnerabilities & Exposure]]

Publishing vulnerability details immediately, with or without vendor cooperation. Controversial.

**Context.** Publish everything immediately, vendor cooperation or not — historically the lever that forced an industry that preferred to bury bugs into actually fixing them. The cost is a window where attackers have details and defenders have no patch. Mostly superseded by coordinated disclosure, but it resurfaces when a vendor stonewalls a researcher long enough.
