# Interoperability

> **Branch:** [[15 - Standards, Formats & Bodies|Standards, Formats & Bodies]]

The ability of different systems or implementations to work together by adhering to shared standards and formats.

**Context.** The whole payoff of standards, and harder than it sounds: a spec with ambiguities or optional features yields implementations that nominally conform yet don't interoperate. Conformance test suites and reference implementations exist to close that gap.
