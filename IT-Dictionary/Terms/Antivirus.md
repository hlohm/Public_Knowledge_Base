# Antivirus

> **Domain:** [[06 - Endpoint and Host Security|Endpoint & Host Security]]
> **Also known as:** AV
> **German:** Virenschutz / Antivirus

Classic signature-based malware detection. Necessary but insufficient on its own.

**Context.** Signatures still earn their keep against the commodity bulk — the mass-mailed Trojans and drive-by junk that make up most malicious volume. What they miss by design: fileless techniques, LotL abuse of signed system tools, and anything novel. Hence the industry's move to EPP/EDR; the built-in Defender on current Windows is genuinely competitive, configured properly.
