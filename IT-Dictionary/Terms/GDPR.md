# GDPR

> **Domain:** [[13 - Compliance and Standards|Compliance & Standards]]
> **Also known as:** General Data Protection Regulation, DSGVO
> **German:** Datenschutz-Grundverordnung (DSGVO)

**G**eneral **D**ata **P**rotection **R**egulation. EU regulation on personal-data processing. Key roles/terms: controller, processor, data subject, DPO, DPIA, SCCs, right to erasure.

**Context.** For IT operations the practical surface is: knowing where personal data lives (records of processing), deleting it on request and on schedule, breach notification within 72 hours, and processor contracts (AVV/DPA) with every vendor that touches the data — including your hosting and your ticket system. Fines scale to global revenue, which got management's attention in a way security never had.
