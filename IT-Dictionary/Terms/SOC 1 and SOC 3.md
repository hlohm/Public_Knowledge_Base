# SOC 1 and SOC 3

> **Domain:** [[13 - Compliance and Standards|Compliance & Standards]]
> **Also known as:** SOC 1, "SOC 3"

**SOC 1** = controls relevant to financial reporting. **SOC 3** = public-facing summary of SOC 2.

**Context.** Pick by audience: SOC 1 goes to your customers' financial auditors (payroll, billing platforms), SOC 3 goes on your website as marketing. Neither substitutes for SOC 2 when a customer's security team is doing vendor review.
