# Toil

> **Branch:** [[12 - DevOps & SRE|DevOps & SRE]]

In SRE, manual, repetitive, automatable operational work that scales linearly with service size and produces no lasting value.

**Context.** Google's SRE practice caps toil (famously ~50%) so engineers have time to automate it away rather than drowning in it. Naming and measuring toil is what justifies the investment in eliminating it — the alternative is ops work growing forever with the system.
