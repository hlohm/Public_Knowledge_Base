# Factor

> **Domain:** [[04 - Identity and Access Management|Identity & Access Management]]
> **German:** Faktor

Something you **know** (password), **have** (token, phone), **are** (biometric), or are **located** (geo/IP).

**Context.** True multi-factor means different *categories* — password plus security question is still one factor, twice. The categories also fail differently: knowledge gets phished, possession gets SIM-swapped or fatigue-bombed, biometrics can't be rotated after a breach. Number matching in push apps and FIDO2 exist precisely because not all second factors are equal.
