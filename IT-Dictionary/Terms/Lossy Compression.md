# Lossy Compression

> **Branch:** [[14 - Graphics, Media & HCI|Graphics, Media & HCI]]

Compression that permanently discards information judged perceptually unimportant to achieve much smaller files (JPEG, MP3, most video).

**Context.** Exploits the limits of human perception — frequencies you can't hear, colour detail your eye downplays. The data is gone for good, so re-encoding compounds the loss (generation loss). The right choice when 'good enough and small' beats 'perfect and huge' — i.e. most media delivery.
