# Overfitting

> **Branch:** [[13 - AI & Machine Learning|AI & Machine Learning]]

When a model learns the training data too well — including its noise — and fails to generalise to new data. Memorisation rather than learning.

**Context.** The central failure mode of ML. The tell is a wide gap: great on training data, poor on held-out test data. The whole toolkit of regularisation, dropout, cross-validation, and 'more data' exists to fight it. The opposite, underfitting, is a model too simple to capture the real pattern.
