# Fail Secure

> **Domain:** [[01 - Core Principles|Core Principles & Models]]
> **Also known as:** Fail Safe

When something breaks, default to denying access. Opposite of *fail open*.

**Context.** 'Fail safe' originally means safe *for the user* (e.g. doors unlock in a fire) — opposite trade-off from 'fail secure'. Choose deliberately.
