# ISO 27001

> **Domain:** [[13 - Compliance and Standards|Compliance & Standards]]
> **Also known as:** ISO/IEC 27001, "ISO 27002", "ISMS"

International standard for an **ISMS** (Information Security Management System). Certifiable. **27002** is the companion control catalog.

**Context.** The certification customers actually ask for in Europe. The certifiable thing is the *management system* — risk assessment, SoA, internal audits, management review — not a fixed checklist; controls come from Annex A (93 controls since the 2022 revision). Expect a multi-month project the first time and an audit cycle every year after.
