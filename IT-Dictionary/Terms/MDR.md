# MDR

> **Domain:** [[06 - Endpoint and Host Security|Endpoint & Host Security]]
> **Also known as:** Managed Detection and Response

**M**anaged **D**etection and **R**esponse. EDR/XDR delivered as a service, with humans in the loop.

**Context.** What you're actually buying is the 24/7 eyes — alerts at 03:00 get triaged while your team sleeps, which no small IT shop can staff internally. Contract diligence: response authority (may they isolate hosts, or only call you?), guaranteed response times, and whether "response" means action or an email. The economics beat building a SOC below a surprisingly large company size.
