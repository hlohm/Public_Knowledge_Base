# PCI DSS

> **Domain:** [[13 - Compliance and Standards|Compliance & Standards]]
> **Also known as:** Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard

**P**ayment **C**ard **I**ndustry **D**ata **S**ecurity **S**tandard. Controls required for handling cardholder data. Prescriptive.

**Context.** Contractual, not legal — enforced by the card brands through your acquiring bank, with fines and ultimately loss of card processing. The scoping game dominates: segment the cardholder-data environment ruthlessly or use tokenizing payment providers so the data never touches your systems, shrinking the audit to a questionnaire.
