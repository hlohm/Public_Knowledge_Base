# Policy Standard Procedure Guideline

> **Domain:** [[02 - Risk and Governance|Risk & Governance]]
> **Also known as:** Policy, "Standard", "Procedure", "Guideline"

A document hierarchy: **policy** (the what and why), **standard** (mandatory specifics), **procedure** (step-by-step how), **guideline** (recommendations).

**Context.** Getting the layer right matters: write specifics into policy and you'll amend it through management approval every time a TLS version dies. Policy says "data in transit must be encrypted", the standard says "TLS 1.2+ with these suites", the procedure says which checkbox to click on the Exchange connector.
