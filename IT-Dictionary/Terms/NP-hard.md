# NP-hard

> **Branch:** [[10 - Theory of Computation|Theory of Computation]]

Problems at least as hard as the hardest problems in NP — every NP problem reduces to them — but not necessarily *in* NP themselves.

**Context.** Broader than NP-complete: an NP-hard problem need not even be decidable. The label is the red flag that exact efficient solutions are (almost certainly) off the table, pushing you toward approximation or heuristics.
