# Threat

> **Domain:** [[02 - Risk and Governance|Risk & Governance]]
> **German:** Bedrohung / Gefährdung

A potential cause of harm to an asset. Often a *threat actor* (who) using a *threat vector* (how).

**Context.** Threats exist independently of your posture — ransomware crews exist whether or not you patch. You control vulnerabilities and impact, not threats; this is why threat intelligence is about awareness while vulnerability management is about action.
