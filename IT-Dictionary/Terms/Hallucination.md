# Hallucination

> **Branch:** [[13 - AI & Machine Learning|AI & Machine Learning]]

When a generative model produces confident, fluent output that is factually wrong or fabricated — plausible-sounding nonsense.

**Context.** Not a bug to be fully patched but a consequence of the objective: the model optimises for plausible continuations, not truth, and has no built-in notion of factual grounding. Mitigations (RAG, citations, tool use, verification) reduce it; it can't be assumed gone. The reason to verify LLM output on anything that matters.
