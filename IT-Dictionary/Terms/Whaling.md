# Whaling

> **Domain:** [[07 - Threats and Attacks|Threats & Attacks]]

Spear-phishing targeted at executives.

**Context.** Spear phishing aimed at the big fish — executives and finance approvers — because their access and authority shorten the path to money or data. Overlaps heavily with BEC. The catch is that the highest-value targets are often the ones who resent security friction, so the win is phishing-resistant MFA plus a payment-verification process that doesn't bend for a "CEO, urgent" email.
