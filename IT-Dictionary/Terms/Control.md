# Control

> **Domain:** [[02 - Risk and Governance|Risk & Governance]]
> **German:** Maßnahme / Kontrolle

A safeguard. Categorized as **preventive**, **detective**, **corrective**, or **deterrent**; and as **administrative** (policy), **technical** (tooling), or **physical**.

**Context.** Frameworks are essentially structured control catalogs: ISO 27002, NIST 800-53, CIS Controls, BSI Grundschutz-Bausteine. A useful habit when designing defenses: check you have coverage across prevent *and* detect *and* respond for a given risk, not three preventive controls and a blind spot.
