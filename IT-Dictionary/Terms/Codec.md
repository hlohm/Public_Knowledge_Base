# Codec

> **Branch:** [[14 - Graphics, Media & HCI|Graphics, Media & HCI]]
> **Also known as:** Coder-Decoder

An algorithm (and its implementation) that **co**des and **dec**odes a media stream — compressing audio or video for storage/transmission and decompressing it for playback (H.264, AV1, AAC, Opus).

**Context.** Distinct from a container format (MP4, MKV), which just wraps the codec's output plus metadata and tracks — a common confusion. Codec choice trades compression efficiency against compute and licensing; the royalty-free AV1/Opus vs. patent-encumbered H.264/H.265 split is a live issue.
