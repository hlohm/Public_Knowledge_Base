# ISO

> **Branch:** [[15 - Standards, Formats & Bodies|Standards, Formats & Bodies]]
> **Also known as:** International Organization for Standardization

The international standards body publishing standards across virtually every field — including ones you meet constantly: ISO 8601 (dates), ISO/IEC 27001 (infosec management), C and C++ language standards.

**Context.** Standards by national-body consensus, which is why they're authoritative but slow and (often) paywalled. The name isn't an acronym — it's from Greek *isos*, 'equal', chosen to be the same across languages. ISO/IEC 27001 is likely the one nearest your security work.
