# IEEE 754

> **Branch:** [[01 - Computing Foundations|Computing Foundations]]

The standard that defines how floating-point numbers are stored and how their arithmetic behaves, including special values like ±∞ and NaN.

**Context.** It's why floats behave identically across CPUs and languages, and why `NaN != NaN`. The single/double precision (32/64-bit) formats almost everything uses come from here.
