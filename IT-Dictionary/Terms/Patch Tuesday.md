# Patch Tuesday

> **Domain:** [[09 - Vulnerabilities and Exposure|Vulnerabilities & Exposure]]

Microsoft's monthly security update cadence. Followed by 'Exploit Wednesday'.

**Context.** The rhythm that structures enterprise patching: second Tuesday, Microsoft (and Adobe, others) ship the month's fixes; "Exploit Wednesday" is the grim follow-up as attackers reverse the patches into working exploits — sometimes within hours. Out-of-band patches break the cadence for the urgent ones. The practical takeaway: have a tested deployment ring ready to move fast on the criticals, especially anything also in KEV.
