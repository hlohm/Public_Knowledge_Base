# NIST SP 800-171

> **Domain:** [[13 - Compliance and Standards|Compliance & Standards]]
> **Also known as:** SP 800-171, "CUI"

Controls for protecting **CUI** (Controlled Unclassified Information) in non-federal systems.

**Context.** Relevant when US government data flows through commercial contracts — including foreign subcontractors. 110 requirements derived from 800-53, self-assessed with scores submitted to a DoD database; CMMC adds the external check.
