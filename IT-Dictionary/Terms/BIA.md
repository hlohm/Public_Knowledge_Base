# BIA

> **Domain:** [[02 - Risk and Governance|Risk & Governance]]

**B**usiness **I**mpact **A**nalysis. Identifies which processes are critical and how long the business can tolerate them being down.

**Context.** The BIA is what turns "everything is critical" into an honest ranking. Its outputs — RTO and RPO per process — are the requirements that size your backup strategy and failover budget. Doing backups without a BIA means guessing at how much loss is acceptable.
