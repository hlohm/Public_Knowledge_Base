# Purple Team

> **Domain:** [[11 - SecOps Detection and Response|SecOps, Detection & Response]]

Collaborative offense + defense exercises designed to *improve detections*, not just score wins.

**Context.** The fix for red-vs-blue theater: run the attack *with* the defenders watching, so every technique either fires a detection or generates a new one on the spot — the deliverable is improved coverage, not a win/loss. Cheaper and more educational than a covert red team, and the natural home of adversary-emulation exercises mapped to ATT&CK.
