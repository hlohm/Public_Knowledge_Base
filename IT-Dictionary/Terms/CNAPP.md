# CNAPP

> **Domain:** [[10 - Cloud and Modern Architecture|Cloud & Modern Architecture]]
> **Also known as:** Cloud-Native Application Protection Platform

**C**loud-**N**ative **A**pplication **P**rotection **P**latform. Convergence platform bundling CSPM + CWPP + CIEM + more.

**Context.** The vendor answer to alert fatigue across CSPM+CWPP+CIEM silos: correlate them, so "internet-exposed VM *with* critical CVE *with* admin role attached" surfaces as one prioritized risk instead of three disconnected findings. Evaluate on correlation quality and runtime depth — the acronym itself guarantees neither.
