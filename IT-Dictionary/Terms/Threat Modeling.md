# Threat Modeling

> **Domain:** [[08 - Application Security|Application Security]]

Structured analysis of what could go wrong with a system. Methodologies: STRIDE, PASTA, LINDDUN, attack trees.

**Context.** The highest-leverage AppSec activity because it finds design flaws no scanner can — and the cheapest, since it's a whiteboard before it's a CVE. Four questions carry it (Shostack): what are we building, what can go wrong, what will we do about it, did we do a good job. Keep it lightweight and repeated, not a one-time 80-page artifact nobody revisits.
