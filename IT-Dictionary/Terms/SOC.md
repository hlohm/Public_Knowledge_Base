# SOC

> **Domain:** [[11 - SecOps Detection and Response|SecOps, Detection & Response]]
> **Also known as:** Security Operations Center

**S**ecurity **O**perations **C**enter. Team and facility that monitors and responds to threats.

**Context.** The build-vs-buy decision most orgs get wrong by underestimating 24/7 staffing: a real around-the-clock SOC needs ~8-12 analysts, which is why MDR and co-managed models dominate below enterprise scale. Whether in-house or outsourced, the SOC is only as good as its detection engineering and the response authority it's actually granted — eyes on glass without the mandate to act is just expensive monitoring.
