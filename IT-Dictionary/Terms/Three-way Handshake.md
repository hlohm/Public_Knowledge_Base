# Three-way Handshake

> **Branch:** [[04 - Networking|Networking]]

TCP's connection setup: SYN, SYN-ACK, ACK — synchronising sequence numbers before data flows.

**Context.** The one round-trip of setup latency that makes short TCP connections feel slow, and the basis of the SYN-flood DoS.
