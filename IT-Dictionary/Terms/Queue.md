# Queue

> **Branch:** [[09 - Algorithms & Data Structures|Algorithms & Data Structures]]
> **German:** Warteschlange

A FIFO (first-in, first-out) collection: enqueue at the back, dequeue from the front.

**Context.** The basis of breadth-first search, task scheduling, buffering, and message brokers. Variants abound: deque (both ends), priority queue (ordered by priority, usually a heap), ring buffer (fixed-size circular).
