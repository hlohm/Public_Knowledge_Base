# Coordinated Disclosure

> **Domain:** [[09 - Vulnerabilities and Exposure|Vulnerabilities & Exposure]]
> **Also known as:** Responsible Disclosure

Researcher reports vuln to vendor, agrees on timeline before public release. Sometimes called **responsible disclosure**.

**Context.** The negotiated middle ground between full disclosure (publish now) and silence: report privately, agree a fix timeline (90 days is the Project-Zero-popularized norm), then go public together. The friction is real — researchers escalate when vendors stall or go quiet, vendors complain about short clocks — which is why having a published security.txt and a responsive process protects you from a surprise 0-day drop.
