# Adversary Emulation

> **Domain:** [[11 - SecOps Detection and Response|SecOps, Detection & Response]]

Simulating a specific real-world threat actor's TTPs (CALDERA, Atomic Red Team).

**Context.** The disciplined version of red teaming: replay a *specific* actor's documented TTPs (mapped to ATT&CK) to test whether your detections actually fire against the threats you claim to defend against. Atomic Red Team gives per-technique tests; CALDERA automates chains. Distinct from a goal-driven red team — emulation is about detection coverage, not winning.
