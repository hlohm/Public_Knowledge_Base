# Chomsky Hierarchy

> **Branch:** [[10 - Theory of Computation|Theory of Computation]]

A four-level classification of formal grammars by expressive power: regular ⊂ context-free ⊂ context-sensitive ⊂ recursively enumerable, each recognised by a more powerful machine.

**Context.** The unifying map of the whole field: it ties grammar classes to the machines that recognise them (regular↔FSM, context-free↔pushdown automaton, unrestricted↔Turing machine). Explains *why* regex can't parse nested code and why language design picks a grammar level deliberately.
