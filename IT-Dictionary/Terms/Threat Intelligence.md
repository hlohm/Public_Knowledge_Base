# Threat Intelligence

> **Domain:** [[11 - SecOps Detection and Response|SecOps, Detection & Response]]
> **Also known as:** CTI, "Cyber Threat Intelligence", "Threat Intel"

Knowledge about adversaries, used to prioritize defenses. **Strategic** / **operational** / **tactical** tiers.

**Context.** Useful only when it changes a decision: strategic intel informs where to invest, operational intel warns of a campaign against your sector, tactical intel (IOCs, TTPs) feeds detection. The failure mode is buying expensive feeds nobody actions — relevance and integration beat volume. For most teams, free sources (CISA, sector ISACs, vendor reporting) plus a clear consumer outrank a pricey platform.
