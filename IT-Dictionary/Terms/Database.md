# Database

> **Branch:** [[06 - Data & Databases|Data & Databases]]
> **German:** Datenbank

An organised collection of structured data, managed by a **DBMS** (database management system) that handles storage, querying, concurrency, and durability.

**Context.** The word is used loosely for both the data and the engine managing it. The fundamental tension running through the whole field: how much structure and consistency you enforce up front versus how much speed and scale you buy by relaxing them.
