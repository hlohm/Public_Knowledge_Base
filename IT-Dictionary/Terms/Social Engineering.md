# Social Engineering

> **Domain:** [[07 - Threats and Attacks|Threats & Attacks]]

Manipulating people rather than systems. Phishing is one technique; pretexting, baiting, tailgating are others.

**Context.** The constant beneath most incidents: it's cheaper to fool a person than to defeat the crypto. The levers are universal — authority, urgency, reciprocity, fear — and they scale from mass phishing to a tailored phone call. Technical controls reduce the blast radius (MFA, least privilege), but process (verify out-of-band, normalize saying "let me call you back") is what closes the human gap.
