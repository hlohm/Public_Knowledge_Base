# CPU

> **Branch:** [[02 - Hardware & Architecture|Hardware & Architecture]]
> **Also known as:** Central Processing Unit, Processor

The component that executes instructions — fetching them from memory, decoding, and performing arithmetic, logic, and control. The 'engine' of a computer.

**Context.** Modern CPUs are wildly more complex than the fetch-decode-execute story: multiple cores, deep pipelines, out-of-order execution and speculation, all reconstructing the illusion of simple sequential execution.
