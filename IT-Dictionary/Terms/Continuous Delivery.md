# Continuous Delivery

> **Branch:** [[12 - DevOps & SRE|DevOps & SRE]]
> **Also known as:** CD, Continuous Deployment

Keeping the main branch *always releasable*: every change flows through an automated pipeline to a deployable artifact, and shipping is a business decision, not an engineering project. **Continuous Deployment** removes even that decision — every green build ships.

**Context.** The forcing function is the point: to make every commit releasable you're compelled into small batches, real test automation, and scripted deploys — the practices that DORA research ties to elite performance. Delivery vs Deployment is one word and one human approval apart; most regulated shops live on the Delivery side.
