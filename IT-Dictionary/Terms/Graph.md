# Graph

> **Branch:** [[09 - Algorithms & Data Structures|Algorithms & Data Structures]]
> **German:** Graph

A set of **vertices** connected by **edges** (directed or undirected, weighted or not) — the general model for networks and relationships.

**Context.** Models almost everything relational: social networks, road maps, dependencies, the web. Represented as an adjacency list (sparse) or matrix (dense). A huge family of algorithms (traversal, shortest path, flow, matching) builds on it; many real problems reduce to a known graph problem.
