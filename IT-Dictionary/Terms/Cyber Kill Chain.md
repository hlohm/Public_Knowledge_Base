# Cyber Kill Chain

> **Domain:** [[11 - SecOps Detection and Response|SecOps, Detection & Response]]

Lockheed Martin's 7-phase intrusion model: Recon → Weaponization → Delivery → Exploitation → Installation → C2 → Actions on Objectives.

**Context.** The original "break any link to stop the attack" model, and still a clean way to explain defense-in-depth to non-specialists. Its limits show against modern intrusions — it's linear, perimeter-centric, and light on the post-breach phases where lateral movement and persistence actually live — which is why ATT&CK largely supplanted it for detection work. Good for narrative, weaker for coverage mapping.
