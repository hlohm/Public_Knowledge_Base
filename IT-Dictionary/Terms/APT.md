# APT

> **Domain:** [[07 - Threats and Attacks|Threats & Attacks]]
> **Also known as:** Advanced Persistent Threat

**A**dvanced **P**ersistent **T**hreat. Well-resourced, often state-aligned adversary with long-term goals. Groups get names like APT29, Lazarus, Fancy Bear.

**Context.** "Advanced" oversells it — APTs use whatever works, often plain phishing — but "persistent" is literal: they intend to stay for months or years and will re-enter after eviction. The defensive implication is that incident response against an APT is a campaign, not a cleanup; assume multiple footholds and watch for re-entry after you think you're done.
