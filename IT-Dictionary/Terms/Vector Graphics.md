# Vector Graphics

> **Branch:** [[14 - Graphics, Media & HCI|Graphics, Media & HCI]]

Images defined by mathematical shapes — points, lines, curves, polygons — rather than a grid of pixels, so they scale to any size without loss of quality.

**Context.** The clean contrast with raster: vectors are resolution-independent and tiny for geometric art (logos, icons, fonts, SVG, CAD), but unsuited to photographs. The rule of thumb: vector for designed shapes, raster for captured reality.

## Often confused with

- [[Raster]] — Raster is a fixed pixel grid (great for photos, blurs when scaled up); vector is math-defined shapes (great for logos/icons, scales infinitely).
