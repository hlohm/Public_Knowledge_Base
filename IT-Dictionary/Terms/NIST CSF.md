# NIST CSF

> **Domain:** [[13 - Compliance and Standards|Compliance & Standards]]
> **Also known as:** NIST Cybersecurity Framework

**C**yber**s**ecurity **F**ramework. Risk-based with six functions: **Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover**. Voluntary, widely adopted.

**Context.** The lingua franca for describing a security program's shape — useful even in Europe because everything maps to it: vendors tag products, frameworks publish crosswalks, and "we're weak on Detect" is a sentence executives understand. Version 2.0 (2024) added Govern as the sixth function.
