# KEV

> **Domain:** [[09 - Vulnerabilities and Exposure|Vulnerabilities & Exposure]]
> **Also known as:** Known Exploited Vulnerabilities

CISA's **K**nown **E**xploited **V**ulnerabilities catalog. If a CVE is in KEV, patch yesterday.

**Context.** The single best free patch-prioritization signal: if a CVE is in CISA's KEV catalog, it is being exploited in the wild *right now* and belongs at the front of the queue regardless of its CVSS. US federal agencies get binding deadlines to remediate KEV entries; everyone else should treat it the same way. Wire it into your vulnerability management as a hard override.
