# Church-Turing Thesis

> **Branch:** [[10 - Theory of Computation|Theory of Computation]]

The hypothesis that any function 'effectively computable' by any means is computable by a Turing machine — equating the intuitive notion of computation with the formal one.

**Context.** A thesis, not a theorem (it relates an informal idea to a formal model, so it can't be proved), but universally accepted. Its force: every reasonable model of computation devised — lambda calculus, recursive functions, real CPUs — has turned out exactly equivalent in power.
